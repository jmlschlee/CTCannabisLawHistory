Where They Stand

Who has backed cannabis access and who has pushed for penalties and enforcement: lawmakers by how they voted, and department heads, police, town officials, advocates and businesses by the positions they filed in testimony. Every count opens the votes or filings behind it.

What “pro” and “anti” mean here

Each cannabis bill is classed from its own statement of purpose as expanding access or easing penalties, adding penalties, restrictions or enforcement, mixed, or regulation and study. A pro-cannabis vote or filing supports a bill of the first kind or opposes one of the second. An anti-cannabis vote or filing does the reverse. Mixed and regulatory bills are left out of these counts, and each bill’s page shows how it was classed and why.
