How pro and anti are decided

Each cannabis bill is marked from its own statement of purpose: expands access or eases penalties, adds penalties, restrictions or enforcement, or mixed. A pro vote or filing supports the first kind or opposes the second; an anti vote or filing does the reverse. Mixed and regulatory bills are not counted. Lawmakers are scored on committee and final votes; everyone else on the positions they filed in testimony. Two thirds one way is pro or anti; anything closer is mixed.
