Who testified

Every named party who filed testimony on a Connecticut cannabis bill, ordered by the number of filings. The same person filing as “Accettullo, Joseph” and as “Joseph R Accettullo” is one person here, and every spelling the record used is shown on their entry.

What a filing count does and does not mean

It counts written positions filed on the public record. It is not a measure of influence, of time spent, or of whether anyone was persuaded. Someone who filed once may have been decisive; someone who filed ten times may have been ignored. The influence page explains the evidence scale in full.
