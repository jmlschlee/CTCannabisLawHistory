Bill lineage

How each bill moved, what was proposed along the way, and what was refused. Amendments that were filed and never called are shown as prominently as the ones that passed: they are the clearest record of what someone asked for and did not get.

What the bill record cannot give

Introducers, committees and LCO numbers, yes. A drafting requester, no. Every cannabis act here was raised by a committee, not a named member, so no individual author appears on the face of the record. Who asked for a given amendment is unknown throughout this dataset and is logged as gap GAP_0003.
