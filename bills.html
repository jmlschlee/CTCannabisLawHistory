Every Connecticut bill from 2012 to 2026 that the General Assembly indexed under marijuana, cannabis or hemp, or whose title names them: how far each got, who sponsored it, who testified for and against it, and how every committee and floor vote went.

Bills that did not pass, ranked by testimony

Only bills whose title is about cannabis or hemp are ranked. Multi-subject budget and omnibus bills are left out, because most of their testimony is about something else.

How positions are counted

Only the position each witness chose when filing counts: the legislature prints it in the file name as “Supports” or “Opposes”. It has done this since about 2019, so older bills show most filings as “no side stated”. Filings marked “support with changes” or about one section only are not counted either way.
