Raised bill to public act — and the ones that died

Bills

How each bill moved, what was proposed along the way, and what was refused. Bills that did not pass are shown beside the ones that did, and amendments that were filed and never called are shown as prominently as the ones that passed: they are the clearest record of what someone asked for and did not get.

A correction worth knowing about

An earlier version of this dataset tracked only the bills that produced a cannabis public act. Eight more were found by working backwards from the testimony record: every one had drawn written testimony on a cannabis question, and none was in the bill table. Four of the eight turned out to have been enacted, which is why this site now carries sixteen public acts rather than twelve. The other four failed and are below.

What the bill record cannot give

Introducers, committees and LCO numbers, yes. A drafting requester, no. Every cannabis act here was raised by a committee rather than a named member, so no individual author appears on the face of the record. Who asked for a given amendment is unknown throughout this dataset and is logged as gap GAP_0003.
