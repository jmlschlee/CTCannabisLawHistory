Influence

What the public record contains about who tried to shape Connecticut cannabis law. Nothing on this page asserts that anyone succeeded, and nothing asserts improper influence by anybody.

Read the scale before you read anything else

The strength rating measures the evidence that the actor did the thing recorded. It never measures whether the action affected an outcome. Registering to lobby, making a disclosed contribution, receiving an award and filing testimony are all lawful, ordinary and public.

Nothing in this dataset reaches strength 5. A 5 would need a document tying an actor directly to a drafting or decision act, and no such document is in the public record examined.
