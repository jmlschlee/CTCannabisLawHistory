Who showed up, and which way

Public hearing testimony

Every written testimony record the committee index lists for every cannabis bill, with the speaker, the organization and the position exactly as filed. Files that could not be retrieved are listed as not retrieved rather than dropped.

What a position field means here

The committee names each testimony file with the filer’s stated position. Where the filename does not carry one, the row reads not stated in the filename rather than a guess. Testifying is participation in a public process; it is not evidence of influence on the outcome.
