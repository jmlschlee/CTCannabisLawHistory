How to read a vote

A Yea is a vote for the bill in front of the committee or chamber on that day. It is not, by itself, a vote “for cannabis”: some bills in a topic expand access and some restrict it, so every count links to the bills behind it. Stance counts use committee votes and final-passage votes only. Amendment votes are listed in each record but not counted, because a Yea on an amendment can help or hurt the bill. Why a member voted is not on a tally sheet; the closest public evidence is what they sponsored, the amendments they offered, the testimony they filed and the floor debate, all linked below. Party is shown only for members on the General Assembly’s current (2026) roster, because older roll calls do not print it.
