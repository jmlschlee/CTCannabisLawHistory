What this site will not do

It does not allege corruption

Testifying, lobbying, donating and attending events are lawful, ordinary and disclosed. The site records what happened and, separately, what followed. It never claims one caused the other.

It does not guess at a gap

Where the record runs out, the site says so and names the document that would close it. Twelve such gaps are logged by name, and each has been checked against the published record.

It does not quote a stale statute

Statutory text comes from the enacted acts, never from the chapter pages, which were still printing a repealed subsection nine months after the repeal took effect.
