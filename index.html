Three things this site will not do

Treat sequence as causation

A contribution, an award or a piece of testimony followed by a favorable decision is two dated public facts. Every influence record carries an alternative explanation and an explicit line saying it records visibility, not causation.

Guess who wrote the language

Bill records give introducers, committees and LCO numbers. They never give a drafting requester. Where authorship is not in the record, the page says unknown and names the earliest source found.

Hide what it cannot show

Twelve open gaps are published with the exact record that would close each, and ten claims are published as things that must not be said, with the safer wording beside them. See the guardrails.
