Connecticut’s adult-use market runs substantially on the instrument that is not law. Requirements including the testing requirements sit in a Department policies and procedures document that was never converted into adopted regulations for the adult-use market. Its version history and the authority cited for each change are not published. That is one of the logged gaps.
