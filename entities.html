Where two names were treated as one

The public record spells the same person several ways. Deciding that “Jerry Farrell” and “Gerald E Farrell” are one man is a judgment, not a fact on the page, so every such judgment is published here with the rule that produced it and how confident it is. Disagree with any of them and the underlying rows are unchanged.

How conservative this is

Two people are merged only when the surname matches and the given names are compatible. Different given names never merge, so Jane Smith and John Smith stay apart. Conflicting middle initials block a merge. An individual is never merged with a campaign committee, so a legislator never becomes their own PAC.

Nothing is destroyed. Every spelling the source used is kept on the entity and shown wherever that entity appears.
