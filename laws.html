What changed

Each section of each act that changed a cannabis rule, with the text it removed beside the text it put in its place. Bracketed words in a Connecticut act are the words the act deletes, which is the only machine-readable record of what the old rule said.

Statutory text here comes from the enacted public acts, not from the General Assembly’s published chapter pages. Those lag the session: on 29 September 2026 the chapter 420h page still printed a subsection that had been repealed nine months earlier. How that is handled.

Pick a subject

Subjects are grouped, because fifteen flat labels are a list, not a way in. Medical, adult-use and hemp are kept apart on purpose: they are governed by different instruments on different timetables, and collapsing them is the commonest error in writing about this.
