Why most of these cannot simply be looked up

A gap here is not a gap in the research. It is a gap in what Connecticut publishes. There is no public conversion register, no published lottery award file, no published version history for the policies and procedures document, and no published record of who requested a given amendment. Those records exist inside the agencies that hold them; none of them is on a website to be fetched.
